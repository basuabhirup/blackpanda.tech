Black Panda Technologies

Black Panda Technologies works with digital marketing agencies to design and maintain white-label automation and software systems, delivered offshore.

Most of the work sits behind the scenes — helping agencies offer automation, AI-assisted workflows, and internal tools to their clients without building an in-house engineering team.

What we actually help agencies with

Agencies usually come to us when:

automation keeps coming up in client conversations

internal workflows are getting messy or manual

they want to offer “AI” or automation, but not as vague add-ons

hiring and maintaining a full technical team doesn’t make sense

The work typically includes:

white-label workflow automation for agency clients

internal automation for the agency itself

custom AI assistants or chat-based tools

frontend-heavy tools and dashboards when needed

adapting open-source systems for specific use cases

Everything is designed to fit your existing service stack, not replace it.

White-label, offshore, and deliberately quiet

Black Panda Technologies operates as an offshore technical partner.

That means:

your agency remains client-facing

the work stays white-label

communication is clean and predictable

delivery focuses on reliability, not visibility

Cost savings naturally come from offshore execution, but the real goal is:

consistency

technical continuity

fewer things breaking when clients scale

How automation is handled here

Automation is treated as infrastructure, not a buzzword.

We standardize workflow maintenance and orchestration using:

make.com

n8n

These tools are chosen because they:

are transparent and inspectable

support complex logic without overengineering

are maintainable by teams over time

AI components are added only where they make sense, and always with:

defined responsibility

fallback logic

human override paths

If something is better solved without AI, that’s the direction we take.

How agency projects usually start

There’s rarely a perfect brief.

It often begins with:

“Clients keep asking for automation”

“We’ve hacked something together and it’s fragile”

“We need a backend partner we don’t have to micro-manage”

The first step is usually:

understanding the agency’s service model

mapping what should be standardized vs customized

deciding what should remain manual

From there, systems are built incrementally and quietly improved.
